Month: October 2026
Theme: Boundaries that hold
Week 1 | 20 | One "no" without guilt | This week, calmly say "no" to one small request - without a long explanation. Then write how you felt before and after saying it.
Week 2 | 20 | 10 minutes just for you | Keep 10 minutes every day only for yourself - no phone, no work. At the end of the week, write what changed.
Week 3 | 20 | Name the feeling | Twice a day, pause and ask "What am I feeling right now?" Write down three feelings you recognised this week.
Week 4 | 30 | One hard conversation, calmly | Say the thing you have been avoiding, in a calm tone - start with "I", not "you". Write what happened.

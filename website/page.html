Questions

Questions people usually ask

What language are the sessions in?

Sessions run in English, Hindi or Hinglish — whichever feels most natural to you.

You do not need to be fluent in either. Speak, ask and express yourself in the language you are most comfortable in. If concepts land better in English but emotions come out in Hindi, Hinglish is perfectly okay.

When it comes to personal growth and relationships, you should never feel limited by language.

“Jis bhasha mein dil khul kar baat ho sake, wahi sabse achhi language hai.”

The focus is not on perfect language. It is on real understanding and honest conversation.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching and therapy are different things.

Coaching works on your present situation — your thoughts, beliefs, patterns, communication and relationships — and above all on what you can do differently from here.

This is not a space for diagnosing or treating mental health conditions, and coaching should never replace professional clinical care. If I feel during a conversation that you need clinical or therapeutic support, I will tell you honestly and point you towards the right professional.

“Har problem ka solution coaching nahi hota — aur sahi help lena weakness nahi, wisdom hai.”

Your emotional safety comes first, always.

What happens in the webinar?

It is for women who know they are capable of more, but somewhere feel stuck, overwhelmed, confused or held back.

You will learn simple, practical ways to shift your mindset, strengthen your confidence, handle emotions better and build healthier relationships. And you can ask your questions directly.

“Aapko sirf motivation nahi, ek nayi direction milegi.”

Come as you are. Leave with a clearer mind and a better way forward.

How long is the program?

Revive, Rise & Relive is a 6-month transformation program built around your mindset, confidence, emotional well-being and relationships.

It also includes one personal 1:1 coaching session with Shashi for focused guidance.

Are sessions online or in person?

The entire program is 100% online, so you can take part from wherever you are.

We connect through a WhatsApp community, meet in interactive webinars, and use Mentie Go for practical challenges and missions.

No travel, no change to your routine — the work happens alongside your real life.

“Bas judna hai, seekhna hai, aur khud ko step-by-step upgrade karna hai.”

Is this only for married women?

Not at all. It is for women aged 18 to 55 — homemaker, working professional, entrepreneur, student, mother, daughter, married, unmarried, or simply at a new turning point in life.

Your growth is not defined by your relationship status or your role.

“Aapki kahani alag ho sakti hai, par growth ka haq sabka hai.”

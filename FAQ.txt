FAQ

1. What language are the sessions in?

The sessions are conducted in English, Hindi, or Hinglish — whichever feels most natural and comfortable to you.

You don’t need to be fluent in English or Hindi. You can speak, ask questions, and express yourself in the language you are most comfortable with.

If you understand concepts better in English but find it easier to express your emotions in Hindi, Hinglish is perfectly okay.

Because when it comes to personal growth and relationships, you should never feel limited by language.

“Jis bhasha mein dil khul kar baat ho sake, wahi sabse achhi language hai.”

Our focus is not on perfect language — it is on real understanding, honest conversations, and meaningful transformation.


2. Is this therapy?

No. Coaching and therapy are different.

Coaching focuses on your present situation, thoughts, beliefs, patterns, communication and relationships — and most importantly, on what you can do differently moving forward.

In these sessions, we will help you understand “What is happening in my life right now?”, identify the patterns that may be holding you back, and work towards practical, realistic changes.

This is not a space for diagnosing or treating mental health conditions, and coaching should never replace professional clinical care.

If during a conversation I feel that you may need clinical or therapeutic support, I will tell you honestly and clearly and guide you towards the appropriate professional.

“Har problem ka solution coaching nahi hota — aur sahi help lena weakness nahi, wisdom hai.”

Your emotional safety and well-being will always come first.

3. What happens in the webinar?

This webinar is for women who know they are capable of more — but somewhere feel stuck, overwhelmed, confused, or held back.

You’ll learn simple, practical ways to shift your mindset, strengthen your confidence, handle emotions better, and create healthier relationships.

And yes — you can ask your questions directly and get a new direction from Coach Shashi Pallava.

“Aapko sirf motivation nahi, ek nayi direction milegi.”

Come as you are. Leave with a clearer mind, stronger confidence, and a better way forward.


4. How long is the program?

Revive, Rise & Relive is a 6-month transformation program designed to help you work on your mindset, confidence, emotional well-being and relationships.

The program also includes one personal 1:1 coaching session with Coach Shashi Pallava for more focused guidance.

3 months. One journey. A stronger version of you.

5. Are sessions online or in person?

The entire program is 100% online, so you can participate from wherever you are.

We connect through a WhatsApp community, attend interactive webinars, and use Mentie Go to complete practical challenges and missions.

No need to change your routine or travel anywhere — your transformation happens alongside your real life.

“Bas judna hai, seekhna hai, aur khud ko step-by-step upgrade karna hai.”

6. Is this only for married women?

This program is for women aged 18–55, whether you are a homemaker, working professional, entrepreneur, student, mother, daughter, married, unmarried — or simply at a new turning point in life.

Because your growth is not defined by your relationship status or role.

“Aapki kahani alag ho sakti hai, par growth ka haq sabka hai.”
